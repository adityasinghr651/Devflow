# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good intent while addressing impact, and focus criticism on ideas and work rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub contact or security reporting channel. Maintainers will review reports confidentially and may remove content or restrict participation. The project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
